Fourteen modules, all optional. Turn on what you need in Settings → Modules.

🤖

AI Chat

Free providers (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama) or paid (Claude, ChatGPT, DeepSeek) — with screen sharing.

🪟

Window Management

Drag windows to snap zones, tile layouts and save arrangements with keyboard shortcuts.

💬

Messenger

Anonymous, encrypted chat on your Wi-Fi or in private rooms.

📋

Clipboard History

Searchable history with pinning and filtering. Never lose a copy again.

🗂️

File Shelf

Drop files in the notch, grab them where you need them. Persistent via security-scoped bookmarks.

📅

Today Tab

Weather, calendar events and battery status at a glance.

🍅

Focus Timer

Built-in Pomodoro with notifications to keep you in flow.

🎚️

Audio Control

🛡️

Search

Notes & Widgets

Why it's useful

The notch is prime real estate at the top of your screen. Most apps ignore it. We use it.

Replace a pile of apps

Clipboard manager, window snapper, shelf, timer, AI client, VPN — one lightweight app instead of six menu-bar icons.

Zero context switching

Everything is one hover away from anywhere. No Dock hunting, no Cmd-Tab, no lost focus.

Private by design

No accounts, servers or tracking. Network calls only go to the AI provider you choose, GitHub, and Open-Meteo for weather.

Yours to shape

Every module is optional. Enable only what you use and keep the rest out of your way.

Free forever

MIT-licensed and open source. Built with Swift and SwiftUI — read it, fork it, improve it.

Makes the notch feel intentional

Turn the one part of your MacBook people complain about into the part you use most.

Without NotchApples

  • A dead black cutout
  • 6 separate utility apps
  • Lost clipboard items
  • Manual window dragging

With NotchApples

  • A live, useful control center
  • One app, modular
  • Searchable, pinned history
  • Snap zones & saved layouts

Get NotchApples

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.

brew tap adityajaindxb/notchapples https://github.com/AdityaJainDXB/NotchApples
brew install --cask notch-apple

or grab the DMG and drag it to Applications.

⬇ Download latest DMG

The app is ad-hoc signed (not notarized), so macOS may ask you to approve it on first launch.
