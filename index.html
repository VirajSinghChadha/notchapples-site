Everything in one place

One notch. Every tool.

Twenty-three modules and add-ons, each optional. Turn on what you use in Settings → Modules, and the rest stays out of your way.

✨

AI that can see your screen

Chat with free AI (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama on your Mac) or bring a key for Claude, ChatGPT or DeepSeek. Ask "what's on my screen?" and it takes a screenshot for you. One-click summarise, translate and fix, plus a full chat history.

🪟

Window snapping

Drag a window up to the notch and drop it on a zone: halves, quarters, thirds. Tile everything at once, save layouts, or use ⌃⌥ + arrows.

📋

Clipboard history

Everything you copy, searchable, pinnable and filterable. Password-manager secrets are never saved.

☀️

Today

Date, local weather, your next calendar events with a "Now" badge, and battery at a glance.

⏱

Focus timer

Pomodoro sessions with the countdown right beside the closed notch, like a Dynamic Island.

New
🧰

Tools

Text Grab copies the text from any part of the screen. Keep your Mac awake for a while, pick any colour on screen and copy its hex code, and a calculator that answers as you type.

💬

Anonymous Messenger

Chat with people on the same Wi-Fi, or create an end-to-end-encrypted room joined with a code. Random handles, no accounts, nothing stored.

Add-on
🪞

Mirror

Check your hair before a call. Flip, zoom and a ring light. The camera stops when you leave the tab.

Add-on
🌍

World Clock

The time in the cities you care about, with day or night and the difference from yours.

🔍

File Search

Spotlight-powered search for files, apps and folders. Return opens, ⌘R reveals, drag results out.

📝

Notes

Quick notes with search, saved automatically on your Mac.

🗂

File Shelf

Drag a file onto the notch to park it. It stays there across restarts. Drag it out wherever you need it.

📡

AirDrop & PairDrop

Send files with AirDrop, or between devices on the same Wi-Fi with a 6-digit code. No server involved.

🔊

Per-app audio

Volume from 0 to 150% and a 10-band EQ for each app, built in on macOS 14.2+.

🛡

Free VPN

A built-in list of free OpenVPN servers, or import your own profiles.

🔐

Face ID-style lock

Lock the notch behind Touch ID, your password, or face unlock with your webcam. Templates stay on your Mac.

🎵

Now Playing

The current track from Music or Spotify, with a tiny equaliser beside the notch.

🧩

Desktop widgets

Small, medium and large widgets with weather, music and your next events, plus the new MacBook Center widget (Small and Medium) for your Mac's stats.

NEWLY ADDED

Translator

Translate between Arabic, English, French, Spanish, Hindi, Mandarin and German, with pronunciation and read-aloud. An add-on: switch it on in Settings → Modules.

NEWLY ADDED
📊

Mac Stats

Live RAM, CPU, network speed, battery and disk space, right in the notch. Also available as a widget in Notification Center.

NEWLY ADDED

Color themes

Private by design

Nothing leaves your Mac
unless you ask.

No account, no tracking

There's nothing to sign up for and no analytics. Your notes, clipboard and chats stay on your Mac.

Your AI, your key

Chats go only to the provider you pick, with your own free or paid key. Ollama keeps everything offline.

Encrypted by default

Messenger rooms are end-to-end encrypted. Keys and face templates live in the macOS Keychain.

Every module optional

Turn off what you don't use and it stops running. Permissions are only asked for when a feature needs them.

Get started

Install Notch apple

Free for macOS 14 Sonoma or newer, on any Mac (notch or not).

1

Download the DMG from the latest GitHub release, or use Homebrew below.

2

Drag it to Applications and open it. macOS may ask you to confirm the first launch.

3

Click the notch or press ⌘E. Pick your modules in Settings.

# with Homebrew
brew tap adityajaindxb/notchapples https://github.com/AdityaJainDXB/NotchApples
brew install --cask notch-apple
Download latest DMG Want a lighter version?

Notch apple is ad-hoc signed (not notarized by Apple), so the first time you open it you may need to right-click → Open.

FAQ

Questions

Does it work on Macs without a notch?

Yes. On Macs without a notch, Notch apple shows a small pill at the top centre of the screen that works exactly the same way.

Is it really free?

Yes, and open source under the MIT licence. The AI features use free providers by default (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter's free models, or Ollama on your Mac). Paid providers are optional and use your own key.

How do I open and close it?

Click the notch, press ⌘E in any app, or drag a file onto it. Press Esc or click elsewhere to close. You can also turn on "open on hover" in Settings → General.

Why does window snapping need Accessibility?

Moving other apps' windows is only possible through macOS Accessibility. Notch apple uses it just to read and set window positions. Turn it on in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

How do updates work?

Notch apple asks GitHub for the latest release. If there's a newer one you'll see "Update available". Choose Update or Not now, or turn off automatic checks in Settings → Updates.

Can I make it smaller?

Turn off modules you don't need in Settings → Modules. Older, smaller builds are listed in the README.
