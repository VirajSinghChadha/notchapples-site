What is Notch apple?

The space you never used,
now the fastest way to think.

Notch apple lives in the black cutout at the top of your MacBook screen (or a small pill on Macs without one). Click it, or press ⌃⌥N, and it opens over whatever you're doing. No windows to find, no app switching. It's free, open source, and everything except Pro works without a code.

Capture anything

See it. Snap it.

Press ⌃⌥S from any app, even full screen. The display under your pointer freezes; drag a box around a question, an error or a chart. Click for the whole display, press Esc or right-click to cancel.

  • Retina-sharp: the crop keeps every pixel, with the exact size shown as you drag
  • A single window (even if others overlap it), the display under your pointer, or every display at once
  • Or paste an image, drop an image or PDF, or use the text you copied or selected
Understand instantly

One click, the right answer.

Pick what you want done. Notch apple takes a quick look at the text on your Mac first and suggests the likely action: Solve for maths, Code for an error, Translate for another language, Explain for a chart.

SolveExplainExplain simplyAnswer onlyHintSummarizeTranslateExtract text · on your MacRewriteCodeAsk

Answers stream in as they're written, with readable maths (not raw LaTeX), code blocks with Copy, and real tables.

Quick actions

Built for the keyboard.

Everything is one click or one shortcut away. Change any global shortcut in Settings.

⌃⌥SCapture from anywhere, even with the notch hidden ⌃⌥NOpen or close the notch ⌘⇧VPaste an image or text as the input ReturnSend · ⌘. Stop · ⌘R Retry ⌘⇧CCopy the answer · ⌘N New task
Follow-up conversations

Keep asking.

"Why are there two answers?" "Explain step 3." "Make it simpler." The AI still sees your capture and its earlier answers, so you never upload it again.

  • Stop keeps whatever was written so far
  • Retry asks again, with a different provider if you switched
  • Edit puts your question back to change and resend
  • Copy one answer, the whole conversation, or export it as Markdown

History

Everything you asked, on your Mac.

Every task is saved locally with its image, mode, question, answer and the model that wrote it. Search "quadratic" and find it again; reopen it and keep going.

  • Search questions, answers, modes and dates
  • Continue, copy, export or delete any task
  • Keep chats forever, 30, 7 or 1 day, keep images or not, or turn history off
Multi-monitor

The right display, every time.

Capture always uses the display your pointer is on, at that display's own resolution, so a Retina laptop next to a 4K or 1080p monitor all come out sharp and correctly sized. Notch apple's own windows are always left out, and it works over full-screen apps and every Space.

Match day, in the notch.

Follow your team with a countdown, fixtures and a league table. Get a notification 30 minutes before kick-off and a flash in the notch on goals and at full time. Click any score for goalscorers, cards and lineups.

  • Club football, the NBA, NFL, MLB and NHL, plus national teams: World Cup, qualifiers, Nations League, Euro, Copa América, Asian Cup and friendlies

Your provider. Your choice.

Use free Google Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter, run models on your own Mac with Ollama (no key, works offline), or bring a key for Claude, ChatGPT or DeepSeek. Test the connection in one click; a badge shows whether the model can read images.

Privacy

Local first. Always clear.

You always know what leaves your Mac.

Stays on your Mac

API keys, chat history, saved captures, and Extract text (Apple's on-device text recognition). History can be turned off, or set to delete itself.

Sent only when you ask

With a cloud provider, only your question, the image you captured or pasted, and that conversation go to the provider you chose. Nothing else.

Ollama: nothing leaves

Choose Ollama and answers are made on your Mac. The notch shows "On this Mac" so you always know.

No tracking, no account

No telemetry. Signing in is optional and only syncs your setup. Every module is optional and stops when off.

FAQ

Questions

Does it work on Macs without a notch?

Yes. On Macs without a notch, Notch apple shows a small pill at the top centre of the screen that works exactly the same way.

Is it really free?

Yes, and open source under the MIT licence. The AI features use free providers by default (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter's free models, or Ollama on your Mac). Paid providers are optional and use your own key.

How do I open and close it?

Click the notch, press ⌃⌥N in any app (⌘E if you used Notch apple before 1.13), or drag a file onto it. Press Esc or click elsewhere to close. You can also turn on "open on hover" in Settings → General.

Why does window snapping need Accessibility?

Moving other apps' windows is only possible through macOS Accessibility. Notch apple uses it just to read and set window positions. Turn it on in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

How do updates work?

Notch apple asks GitHub for the latest release. If there's a newer one you'll see "Update available". Choose Update or Not now, or turn off automatic checks in Settings → Updates.

Can I make it smaller?

Turn off modules you don't need in Settings → Modules. Older, smaller builds are listed in the README.

What does capture need?

Screen Recording permission, asked the first time you press ⌃⌥S. macOS applies it after Notch apple restarts. Reading selected text needs Accessibility; you can always copy text and paste it instead.

Which AI should I use?

Google Gemini is free and reads images. For complete privacy, install Ollama and run ollama pull gemma3:4b: answers are made on your Mac, offline. Settings → AI → Test connection checks your setup.
