Terms and refunds

Last updated 4 October 2026. Plain English, because this is a one-person project run by a high school developer.

What you buy

  • A Pro key ($1) or an Ultimate key ($5) unlocks that tier's features in Notch apple, for life. It's a one-time payment: no subscription, renewal or expiry.
  • Every 1.x update is included. If there is ever a 2.0, you'll be told well in advance whether it costs anything; your current version keeps working regardless.
  • Ultimate includes everything in Pro. Ultimate features marked "coming" arrive in later free updates.
  • A key works on up to 3 of your own Macs. Please don't share it publicly: shared keys may be turned off and reissued to the buyer.
  • Upgrading from Pro to Ultimate costs the $4 difference.

Paying

  • Payments are in Litecoin, sent straight to the developer's wallet. Each order has a unique exact amount; that's how the payment is matched to you.
  • Network and exchange fees are paid by the sender. If the wrong amount arrives, open a request and it will be sorted out.
  • Prices don't include tax. You're responsible for any taxes that apply where you live.

Refunds

Refunds are handled case by case. Open a request with your transaction ID (don't post your email or key publicly). If a refund is given, it's paid in Litecoin to an address you choose, and the key is turned off.

Privacy

  • The app has no ads, analytics or tracking.
  • At checkout, your email is used only to send your key and for "Lost my key?". It's kept in plain text only until the payment arrives (at most 48 hours); after that only a salted one-way hash is stored, which is enough to recognise you when you ask for your key again.
  • Activating a key sends the key and a one-way hash of your Mac, salted per key, to enforce the 3-Mac limit. About once a day, while a key is active, the app downloads the signed list of turned-off keys. Nothing about you is sent with it.
  • The website shows ads to help support a high school developer. They're only on the website, never in the app.

The software

Notch apple is open source (MIT licence) and provided as is, without warranty. Keys unlock features in the official builds.
